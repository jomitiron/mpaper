FAQ

Questions drivers ask

Which papers can MotoPapers renew?

Your vehicle licence, your insurance (third party or comprehensive) and your roadworthiness certificate. You can track all three for every car on your account.

How do the reminders work?

Once a paper's expiry date is on file, we send reminders by SMS and email in the weeks before it runs out, so you have time to renew without rushing.

How do I pay for a renewal?

Top up your MotoPapers wallet by card or bank transfer through Paystack, then pay for renewals from your wallet. You can also pay by card at checkout.

Do I have to type in all my car's details?

No. Upload a photo or PDF of your vehicle licence and we fill in the plate number, make, model, colour, engine and chassis numbers. You just check them.

How do I get my new papers?

We process the renewal and deliver the new papers to the address on your account. You can follow progress in the app.
